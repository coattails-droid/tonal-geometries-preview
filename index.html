About

Tonal Geometries is Series 6 from Calvin Fought, working as Coattails. It’s a collection of 150 generative audiovisual works stored entirely on Ethereum.

Each piece is a sphere of luminous sand, shaped by the vibration patterns first traced by Ernst Chladni on a bowed steel plate. A melody plays, and with every note the sand glides into a new standing-wave pattern, so you can see the music as well as hear it. A larger echo of the same sphere surrounds the viewer, and the two worlds turn slowly in opposite directions, one inside the other.

Every token’s hash composes its own song. It picks a scale from pentatonic, bright diatonic and overtone families, a historical tuning, a starting pitch, a melodic path and contour, and a second voice that sings alongside. Colour moves through the warm Sunlight palette, from gold and amber to magenta and violet. Within the 150, no two pieces share the same combination of traits.

Drag to spin it, then press S or double-tap to listen.

Technical brief

  • Contract: Tonal Geometries (TONGEO), ERC-721 on Ethereum mainnet at 0xDa3Ed34Ba176086BcED1F17cc5f0feA4C355EE2f, with a separate traits contract. Licensed under MIT.
  • Supply and price: 150 tokens at 0.001 ETH each, one per transaction. The ERC-2981 royalty is 10%, with a hard cap of 10%.
  • On-chain art: the full HTML/WebGL page is stored on-chain across code chunks. Each token’s seed is stored at mint and read back with tokenSeed(id), and tokenURI builds the metadata and page entirely from contract data.
  • Uniqueness: a fingerprint check at mint compares each new token’s full trait set. On a collision the seed is rerolled deterministically, up to 32 times, which guarantees 150 unique trait combinations.
  • Traits derived from the seed:
    • Scale Family: Pentatonic, Diatonic Bright or Overtone.
    • Scale, one of six: Major pentatonic, Egyptian pentatonic, Major pentatonic mode 4 (Zhi / Yo), Lydian, Ionian or Lydian dominant.
    • Tuning: 12-TET, 5-Limit Just, Pythagorean or 1/4-Comma Meantone.
    • Base Pitch: the first note heard, with its frequency in Hz.
    • Interval Path: Arpeggio, Stepwise Rise, Stepwise Fall, Pedal Tone or Degree-Fifths Ladder.
    • Contour: Prime, Retrograde or Inversion.
    • States: 3 to 8 vibration patterns per loop.
    • Second Voice: one of five harmony rules.
  • Traits shared by every token: Palette Sunlight, Color Mapping “Along Line + Per State”, Color Drift Medium, Structure Single Square, plus Artist “Calvin Fought”, Moniker “Coattails” and Series “6”.
  • Rendering: the page renders in real-time WebGL with Web Audio synthesis, which needs no outside files or libraries. The inner sphere and a 5× background sphere counter-rotate at 3° per second each. Each note swells in and fades out smoothly. Drag rotates the view, and S or a double-tap toggles sound.
